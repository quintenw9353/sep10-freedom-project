# Content
Topic: **Drawing**
## Overview / Introduction / Context
* Drawing is a familiar pastime many people enjoy during their free time. It
 also serves as a way for individuals to explore their creativity, discover their
 strengths, and see what they might be capable of achieving.

* When used with technology, digital tools, or traditional physical
 materials, drawing generally brings more benefits than drawbacks.
